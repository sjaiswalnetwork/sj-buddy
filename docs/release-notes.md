**SJ Buddy v1.0** - your desktop toon companion for Windows. Made by SJ, built with Claude Fable 5.1.

**Download `SJ-Buddy-v1.0.zip` below**, extract it anywhere, double-click `SJ Buddy.exe`. No installation, no internet. Windows 10 / 11.

If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info** and then **Run anyway** (the app is unsigned; the source is in this repository and `build.bat` rebuilds it).

**In the box**
- `SJ Buddy.exe` (single file, about 320 KB)
- `SJ Buddy - Guide.pdf`
- `README.txt`
- `source/` with a one-click `build.bat`

**Highlights**
- Five characters drawn in code: Shinchan, SpongeBob, Doraemon, Jethalal, Bhide
- Reacts to typing and to the mouse; sleeps when you are away
- Water reminder every 30 minutes (adjustable) with sip tracking and streaks
- About 190 lines per character in English, Hindi and Hinglish
- Learns your routine (start time, lunch hour, typing speed, chattiness, reminder style) and levels up
- Tray icon, Ctrl+Alt+B, start with Windows, custom lines via `my-lines.txt`
- Fully offline; no keyboard hook; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere
