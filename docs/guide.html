SJ Buddy Made by SJ

Your desktop toon companion - Shinchan, SpongeBob, Doraemon, Jethalal and Bhide on your screen. Reacts when you type, reminds you to drink water, learns your routine, and jokes in English, Hindi and Hinglish.

Get it running in 30 seconds

  1. Extract the zip anywhere - your Desktop is fine. You only need the file SJ Buddy.exe; the other files are the guide and the source code.
  2. Double-click SJ Buddy.exe. No installation, no internet, nothing to download. Works on Windows 10 and 11.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info and then Run anyway. The app is brand new and unsigned, so SmartScreen is cautious. It is completely offline and its full source code is included. If an antivirus quarantines it, choose "Restore / Allow" - it is a false positive on a small unsigned program.
  4. Pick your character, give it a name, type your own name, choose the water interval and tick Start with Windows if you want it there every morning. Click Let's go!

Controls

Left-click the buddyIt talks and reacts. During a water reminder a click means "I drank" and logs a sip.
Double-clickPet it. It loves that (and remembers).
DragMove it anywhere on the screen. Its position is remembered.
Right-clickThe full menu: talk, pet, log water, fun actions, change character, rename, language, water interval, talkativeness, size, settings, start with Windows, always on top, sounds, wander, break reminders, stats, about, hide, exit.
Move the mouseIts eyes follow the cursor and it leans toward it. Hover over it and it blushes and says hello. Zoom the mouse past it and it flinches.
Tray icon (near the clock)Left-click shows the buddy, right-click opens the same menu, double-click hides / shows it. Water reminders also appear as Windows notifications while it is hidden.
Ctrl + Alt + BHide / show the buddy from anywhere.
Close itRight-click → Exit (or from the tray icon). To keep it in the tray instead, choose Hide.

What it does on its own

Reacts to typing and to the mouse
Opens its little laptop and types along when you really type (a stray key or mouse scrolling does not fool it), cheers when you type fast (compared with your usual speed), follows the cursor with its eyes, rests with calm eyes when you pause, sleeps with Zzz when you are away, and welcomes you back.
Water reminders
Every 30 minutes by default (15 to 90, or off). Waits if you are away, nudges again if you ignore it, gets serious if you keep ignoring it. Tracks sips per day, average, best day and streak.
Random actions
Blinks, looks around, yawns, stretches, waves, jumps, dances, spins (and gets dizzy), walks along the taskbar, sneezes, laughs, thinks, eats its favourite food, peeks, sings, exercises, checks its phone - plus character specials: Doraemon's bamboo copter, Anywhere Door and mouse panic; SpongeBob's jellyfishing, patty flipping and bubbles; Shinchan's buri-buri dance, Chocobi, Action Kamen and Shiro; Jethalal's chai, garba and "Hey Maa Mataji!"; Bhide's scooter Sakharam, tuition board, society register and Madhavi's achar.
Talks in character
Over 900 lines (about 180 per character, each in English, Hindi and Hinglish, or a mix): greetings by time of day, typing reactions, water lines, click, pet and hover reactions, punchlines, caring lines on bad days, festival wishes (Holi, Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi, Republic Day, Independence Day, Children's Day, Christmas, New Year) and your birthday.
Learns your routine
Usual start and end of your day, lunch hour, peak hours, normal typing speed, longest sessions, late nights, how fast you answer reminders, and how chatty you like it. Click a line you like and it does more of those; hide it right after a line and it learns to talk less.
Levels up
XP from sips (+10), pets (+3), clicks (+2), typing minutes (+1) and breaks (+5). Titles go from Newbie to Buddy, Yaar, Pakka Dost, Jigri Dost, Bestie, Parivaar, Legend, Guru and Desktop Ka Raja. See everything under Stats and what I learned.

Privacy

It never records what you type. To know that you are typing it only checks whether typing keys are currently pressed and counts the presses; nothing is stored, logged or sent anywhere. There is no keyboard hook, no internet access and no account. Its only files are settings.txt and brain.txt in %APPDATA%\SJ Buddy. Delete that folder to reset everything.

Add your own lines

Create a text file named my-lines.txt next to SJ Buddy.exe (or in %APPDATA%\SJ Buddy). One entry per line:

character|category|english|hindi|hinglish

  • character: shinchan, spongebob, doraemon, jethalal, bhide or * for all
  • category: quip, care, wisdom, water, water_ack, water_nag, click, pet, greet_morning, greet_afternoon, greet_evening, greet_night, typing_start, typing_fast, idle_short, lunch, late_night ...
  • Placeholders you can use: {name} (the buddy), {user} (you), {sips}, {streak}, {level}

Example: jethalal|quip|Bapuji is watching. Work!|बापूजी देख रहे हैं। काम करो!|Bapuji dekh rahe hain. Kaam karo!

Extras

"SJ Buddy.exe" --settingsOpens the Settings window (also works while it is already running).
"SJ Buddy.exe" --statsOpens the Stats window.
Pendrive modePut an empty file named portable.txt next to the exe and it keeps its settings next to the exe instead of in %APPDATA%.
Rebuild from sourceRun build.bat in the source folder. It needs only Windows itself (the C# compiler that ships with .NET Framework 4.x).
Tip: the first day it knows nothing about you. After two or three days it will greet you at your usual start time, notice when you work later than usual, and suggest lunch around the time you actually take it.
